# Full-day rehearsal

Use the adaptive schedule in `OPERATING-BRIEF.md` with these explicit simulation events:

- Begin with a context session in which a facilitator explains the user and success
  criteria but leaves implementation choices open.
- Hold two chaperone check-ins: slice alignment after system mapping, then a midpoint
  reality check that includes one piece of corrective feedback.
- Take a lunch-sized interruption and resume by restating current state and next proof.
- Make a green/yellow/red scope decision with roughly 90 minutes remaining.
- Protect at least 45 minutes for the final demo, but allow correctness work to continue
  if the main path is broken.
- After the demo and questions, complete the scorecard as a structured debrief and name
  the two highest-leverage workflow changes.

The rehearsal tests judgment and collaboration, not just typing endurance.
