# Challenge: Conflicting evidence escalation

Operations has introduced a `conflicting-record` evidence marker. Such cases must
never be auto-approved, even when all required evidence is present and the amount is
within policy. Reviewers need to distinguish this condition from ordinary missing
evidence and see a defensible evidence trail.

Extend the service and review console across their repository boundary. Decide what
the API contract should communicate, preserve current behavior where appropriate,
and make the dangerous path visible and testable.

## Acceptance examples

- The ordinary complete request still auto-approves.
- A complete request containing `conflicting-record` is escalated.
- The reviewer surface distinguishes conflict from routine review.
- The result retains useful citations and has a tested boundary case.

You have incomplete requirements by design. Use the product and engineering
chaperones to resolve the ambiguity that matters most.
