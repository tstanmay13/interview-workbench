# Challenge: Failure-safe evidence

The operations team has learned that evidence labels are inconsistent. Requests may
contain duplicates, mixed casing, or blank labels. Reviewers cannot currently see
which required evidence is missing, and malformed evidence has produced confusing
decisions.

Improve the system so that a reviewer can understand and act on incomplete evidence
without weakening the existing auto-approval path.

## Outcome

A valid, complete request should still be easy to approve. An incomplete or malformed
request should fail safely and tell a reviewer what is missing. The reviewer surface
must expose the useful explanation.

## Constraints

- The case service and review console are separate repositories.
- Preserve evidence citations for decisions that succeed.
- Do not add a package dependency unless you can defend its value.
- You may clarify product behavior with your two chaperones.

## Acceptance examples

- `['invoice', 'manager-approval']` within the limit remains auto-approved.
- `[' Invoice ', 'INVOICE']` is not treated as manager approval.
- A reviewer can identify missing evidence without reading server logs.
- Existing callers receive either compatible behavior or an explicitly justified
  contract change.

The examples are not a complete specification. State your assumptions and choose the
smallest valuable slice you can demonstrate with evidence.
